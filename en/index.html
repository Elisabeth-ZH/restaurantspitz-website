TheRestaurant

Lunch & Dinnerup to 45 peopleAll other events

Our cuisine is both creative and down-to-earth, deeply connected to nature. The focus lies on seasonal ingredients from the Alpine region. The result is honest, powerful food – rustic in character, refined in detail. We serve distinctive wines with personality to match. That’s how we do things here.

TheTerrace

Urban and unforgettable. Our terrace is a green oasis in the middle of Zurich – right next to the main train station and yet wonderfully quiet. Surrounded by lush greenery, it provides a unique setting for relaxed summer moments at Spitz. A laid-back lunch, an atmospheric dinner, or a stylish outdoor event – this special place is made for every occasion.

OurBar

The bar at Spitz takes you on a journey through the world of spirits. We are especially proud of our centerpiece: an exquisite whisky collection featuring over 250 bottles, including rare gems steeped in history. Additionally, our creative cocktail selection offers the perfect indulgence for every mood.

For groups of 10 or more we are happy to reserve a space for you. A minimum spend of CHF 35 per person applies.

  • Bar menu
  • Spirits menu

TheBistro

Enjoy an excellent espresso after an exhibition or meet friends for a quick chat at the main train station? In our museum bistro, we offer a selection of simple and affordable food and drinks. Speed is important to us, but our focus is always on flavour. Most of our products are homemade.

YourEvent

Event request

Our services include consulting, planning and executing a wide range of events, both corporate and private. These include corporate events, cultural gatherings, conferences, as well as private occasions such as birthdays and weddings.

Do you need more information or assistance? We’ll be happy to help: anlass@restaurantspitz.ch, phone 044 221 94 78
