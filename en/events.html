Your event at Spitz

Corporate event, apéro, wedding or conference: we advise, plan and run your event. For 10 to 500 guests, right in the centre of Zurich.

Why choose us?

Central location in a museum setting

Located in the heart of Zurich, Restaurant Spitz combines urban flair with a cultural atmosphere. Set within the Swiss National Museum, it offers a special backdrop that gives every event an inspiring touch, whether business or private.

Cuisine & signature drinks

Our kitchen stands for seasonal ingredients, creative compositions and the highest quality. The experience is rounded off by carefully crafted signature drinks, tailored to your event: from elegant to surprising.

Flexible rooms & sizes

Whether a small gathering or a large party: our rooms can be combined individually and adapted to your needs. Different settings make every kind of event possible: stylish, uncomplicated and exactly as you imagine it.

Personal support & planning

From the first enquiry to the last guest, we are at your side. Our experienced team supports you with planning, schedule and details, so you can focus entirely on your guests.
